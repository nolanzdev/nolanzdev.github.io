I rebuilt Trazia. The new version does one thing: **it turns your photo into a coloring page you can print**. Your dog, your kids, grandma's garden: upload a photo, see the coloring page in about 30 seconds, and pay only if you love it.

It also sells ready-made themed coloring books as PDFs that you download and print right away.

## How it works

1. **Upload photos**: up to five at once. Pick the simple style for little kids (big outlines, big spaces to fill) or the detailed one for grown-ups.
2. **Preview for free**: each page is drawn in about 30 seconds and shown with a watermark. Don't like one? Redo it, or drop it.
3. **Pay and print**: pay only for the pages you want to keep. You get high-res PNGs without the watermark, plus a print-ready PDF in A4 and US Letter. Print as many copies as you like.

No account needed: enter your email at checkout. The download link goes to that email, so you can download again later.

## What people use it for

- **Kids**: turn the family pet or the kids themselves into coloring pages, more fun than a store-bought book
- **Birthday parties**: make a page from the birthday kid's photo and print a stack for the guests
- **Gifts**: pet owners and grandparents love them
- **Classrooms**: teachers turn class-trip photos into coloring sheets

## Ready-made coloring books

Don't want to use your own photos? Buy a themed coloring book. The first one is **Dino Adventure**: 24 friendly dinosaurs with big, clear outlines for kids aged 4–9. Every book has sample pages you can look at before buying, and more themes are on the way.

## Why the change

The old Trazia was a free coloring-page generator plus a programmatic-SEO site with 13 languages and 500+ pages, meant to earn from search traffic and credits (old post [here](/posts/trazia/)). But a free tool mostly attracts people who don't plan to pay, and SEO pages take a long time to rank.

This time I started from the other end: who is happy to pay for a coloring page?

- **A coloring page of your own photo is personal.** Nobody else can make a coloring page of your dog, which makes it worth paying for in a way generic themes aren't.
- **See the result before paying.** You can tell at a glance whether the AI did a good job, so there's no need for top-ups or subscriptions first.
- **One-time purchase, no signup.** Most buyers have a one-off need; accounts, credits and subscriptions just get in the way.
- **English only.** The target buyers are parents and gift-givers in the US and Europe, so I'm doing one language well first.

So everything old is gone: the free generator, credits, subscriptions, accounts, the community and the multilingual pages. Old links 301-redirect to the new home page.

## A few notes

- Your photos are used only to draw your pages. They're never published or used for anything else; email us to delete them anytime.
- Photos that work best: well lit, with one to three people or pets that are big and in focus. Busy group shots and dark or blurry photos lose detail.
- Checkout is handled by Waffo Pancake, prices are in US dollars; see the site for current prices. If something's wrong with your file after you pay, email within 14 days and we'll fix it or refund you.
