## How it works

After you upload, RepliReel works through five steps:

1. **Breaks down the original** — segments, shots, characters, scenes, lines, selling points and the opening hook
2. **Adapts it to your product** — same structure and segment lengths, with the product, cast, scenes and lines rewritten for you
3. **Draws reference images** — one per character, scene and prop, so the same person and objects appear in every clip; characters are newly designed for your script, not copies of the real people in the original
4. **Generates video clips** — up to 15 seconds each, in parallel
5. **Assembles the final ad** — play it online or download it

## Two modes

| Mode | Best for | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Step by step** (default) | Tailoring the story to your product | First suggests several adaptation directions to pick from, then writes the detailed script and pauses so you can edit each segment's visuals, lines and length; approve each reference image or redraw it |
| **Auto** | Getting a finished ad fast when the original story already fits | Remakes the original story as is — upload and get the final ad, no babysitting |

Every final ad, character image and scene image you generate is saved in "My works", ready to reuse in your next project. Once an ad is done, you can also "Try another direction": it reuses the breakdown and makes another version.
