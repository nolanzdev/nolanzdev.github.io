[RepliReel](https://replireel.com/en) has changed quite a bit since launch, mostly based on user feedback. Quick recap: upload a winning ad and a photo of your product, and RepliReel remakes the ad with your product, following the original's story and pacing (see the [intro post](/posts/replireel/)). Here's what's new.

> 💡 Visit: <https://replireel.com/en>
{: .prompt-tip }

## 1. Pick a direction before the script

Step-by-step mode used to go straight from the breakdown to one adapted script. Now it first suggests several directions:

- **Close to the original**: same structure and story, just your product
- **New setting**, **new characters**, **new opening hook**, **different selling-point focus**

Each direction spells out the characters, setting, story and selling points, plus what it keeps from the original, what it changes and any risks. If none fit, write your own. The detailed script comes after you pick, and you can still edit every segment's visuals, lines and length.

Step by step is now the default mode. Switch to Auto if you just want the "close to the original" version made end to end.

## 2. Try another direction

Finished an ad and want a different take, or a few variants to test? On the script page, click "Try another direction" and pick another one (or write your own). A new project is created automatically. It **reuses the original breakdown, with no re-analysis**, and runs all the way to the final ad.

One winning ad can turn into several different ads quickly.

## 3. Two video models: MiniMax and Seedance

Each project can choose its video model, and the two have different strengths. The model is labelled on the project page, every clip, the final ad and My works, and you can switch models when re-shooting a clip.

Not sure which to pick? Every example on the [Showcase page](https://replireel.com/en/showcase) is labelled with its model. You can now open the examples, too, and walk through every step read-only: breakdown, script, references and final ad.

## 4. Fix things before you confirm the final ad

In step-by-step mode, the final ad isn't locked straight away. If a character or scene isn't right, change its reference image and regenerate the affected clip. When you're happy, click "Confirm final ad" to lock it. After that it can't be changed; for a different result, use "Try another direction".

Auto mode locks the ad as soon as it's done.

## 5. More natural footage, more consistent characters

- **No copying real people from the original**: characters are newly designed for your script, each with their own voice
- **Consistent outfits and seasons**: each character's full outfit is written into the script and kept in every clip; clothing and scene season follow the product (no summer scenes in a down-jacket ad)
- **Less "AI look" and skin smoothing**: quality requirements for reference images and video prompts, and style words that cause a plastic look are filtered out

## 6. Smaller improvements

- Projects created from a share link keep the original link, so you can open it for comparison any time
- Each step shows how long it took, with a live timer while it runs
- If a clip's download is interrupted, it's retried in the background, with no need to regenerate
- Pay with Alipay, WeChat Pay, or in US dollars
- Better mobile layout with a collapsible menu

## Pricing

Still pay per use: you're only charged when a step succeeds, and failed steps are refunded automatically. See the [Pricing page](https://replireel.com/en/pricing) for current prices.
